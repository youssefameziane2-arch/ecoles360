Contact-source breakdown — horizontal share bars (origine des contacts).

```jsx
<SourceBars data={[
  {label:'WhatsApp', value:128, tone:'green'},
  {label:'Appel', value:64, tone:'navy'},
  {label:'Formulaire', value:42, tone:'amber'},
  {label:'Fiche', value:18, tone:'red'},
]} />
```

Shares are computed from the totals. Tones rotate automatically if omitted.
