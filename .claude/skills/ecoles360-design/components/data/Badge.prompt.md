Status badge for establishment/request/credit states. Soft-tint by default, solid for emphasis.

```jsx
<Badge tone="red">Sponsorisé</Badge>
<Badge tone="green" icon="✓">Vérifié</Badge>
<Badge tone="amber" dot>Solde bas</Badge>
<Badge tone="navy" solid>Nouveau</Badge>
```

Tones: red, navy, green, amber, grey. Status copy is short French (Nouveau, En cours, Traité, Payée, En attente).
